Freshly – Homegrown Marketplace
Freshly is a modern location-based C2C marketplace application that connects local sellers, artisans, farmers, and organic producers directly with nearby buyers. The platform enables users to buy and sell fresh homegrown and locally produced products through a seamless, secure, and community-driven marketplace experience.
